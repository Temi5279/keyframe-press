Keyframe Awards Announces Skew as Official Founding Partner

Partnership Highlights

Partner Skew
Role Founding Partner
Presented Brackets Meme & Viral Content
Inquiries press@keyframe-awards.com

The Keyframe Awards is excited to officially introduce Skew as an inaugural Founding Partner for the 2026 season. The Keyframe Awards celebrates global digital culture, honoring excellence across Memes, Music, Football, Live Streaming, Video Editing, Gaming, Movies, TV Series, and Content Creators.

Spotlighting Digital Humor & Creator Culture

From viral short-form clips to pop-culture commentary, internet humor defines modern digital media. Recognizing the talent driving this space, Skew joins as a Founding Partner to sponsor and present three dedicated meme categories.

Categories Presented by Skew

  • Funniest Viral Meme: Celebrating the sharpest creator content that captured internet culture.
  • Most Relatable Meme: Honoring seamless moments that transform everyday scenarios into viral relatable posts.
  • Best Roast Meme (Presented by Skew): Recognizing precision timing, satirical edits, and top-tier humor.
"Digital content spans everything from live gaming streams and sports highlights to movies and viral memes. Partnering with Skew allows us to directly celebrate the creators keeping the internet entertained."

Celebrating All Pillars of Digital Media

Whether creators focus on sports skill edits, music videos, movie montages, streaming moments, or viral memes, the Keyframe Awards provides formal industry recognition across every digital creative space.
