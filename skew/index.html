Keyframe Awards Announces Skew as Official Founding Partner

Partnership Highlights

Partner Skew
Role Founding Partner

Inquiries press@keyframe-awards.com

The Keyframe Awards is excited to officially introduce Skew as an inaugural Founding Partner for the 2026 award season. As digital video editing continues to reshape global pop culture, Skew joins the movement to recognize and celebrate the creative talent behind viral visual media.

Championing Internet Humor & Viral Editing

Short-form video editing and meme craft are among the most dynamic art forms on the modern internet. Transforming simple clips into culturally defining moments requires precise pacing, timing, and creative audio synchronization.

Through this partnership, Skew will directly sponsor and present three dedicated categories celebrating peak digital satire and humor editing.

Categories Presented by Skew

  • Funniest Viral Meme: Celebrating the sharpest edit that captured global internet culture.
  • Most Relatable Meme: Honoring seamless editing that transforms everyday scenarios into viral relatable art.
  • Best Roast Meme (Presented by Skew): Recognizing precision editing, satirical timing, and legendary humor edits.
"Digital humor relies entirely on the editor's instincts. Partnering with Skew allows us to shine a light on the editors who keep the entire internet laughing."

Empowering the Creator Community

By spotlighting independent meme creators and short-form video editors alongside traditional visual artists, this partnership reinforces the Keyframe Awards' core mission: ensuring every tier of digital visual editing receives formal industry recognition.
